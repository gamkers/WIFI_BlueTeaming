WiFi BlueTeaming Pet

A tiny robot pet that sits on your desk and watches the WiFi around you. When someone launches an attack, it reacts, tells you exactly what's happening and what to do about it. It only listens. It never transmits.

What it catches

Every detection can be switched on or off from the on-screen settings, and each one has its own alert animation, details page and advice page.

D

Deauth attacks

Floods that kick devices off WiFi. Shows attacker, target and router MAC, reason code, peak rate.

2

Evil twin + combo

A copy of your network with a different MAC, and deauth used to push you onto it.

K

Password capture

A handshake grabbed right after a deauth: someone is trying to crack your WiFi password.

P

Karma / Pineapple

One device pretending to be every network your phone remembers.

F

Auth & beacon floods

Fake devices hammering a router, or a spam list of fake WiFi names.

T

Hack tools

Pwnagotchi, WiFi Pineapple, Deauther, Marauder and more by their signatures.

C

Hidden cameras

WiFi cameras by maker, name, or ESP32 boards streaming lots of data.

A

Drones

WiFi Remote ID broadcasts, DJI signals and drone WiFi names.

H

Hunt mode

A metal-detector style meter (and LED) to walk towards the attacker.

Pet mode

Turn on Pet face in settings and the radar becomes a robot face. It looks around while scanning, glances at new devices, and pulls a different face for every attack before showing the details. Prefer the classic radar? It's the default. Click a face to preview it.

Good to know

2.4 GHz only

The ESP8266 can't hear 5 GHz WiFi, so attacks there go unseen.

One channel at a time

While locked on an attacked channel, other channels aren't watched.

False alarms

Mesh WiFi, busy places and your own cameras can trigger alerts. Switch detections off as needed.

No Bluetooth

Bluetooth trackers and Bluetooth-only drone IDs need an ESP32.

Defensive use only. This project only listens. Use it on networks you own or are allowed to monitor.
