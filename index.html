WiFi BlueTeaming

ESP8266 deauth & WiFi attack detector with an OLED display, by Gamkers. Passive: it only listens and never transmits.

How to flash: use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial is needed). Plug in the board with a USB data cable, click Install, pick the serial port and wait for it to finish. If no port shows up, install the CH340 or CP2102 USB driver for your board.

Supported boards

  1. NodeMCU 1.0 (ESP-12E / ESP-12F), 4 MB flash
  2. LOLIN / WEMOS D1 mini, 4 MB flash

ESP32 and ESP-01 are not supported by these binaries.
